Plan drift is hard to notice early

Hiring, churn, sales velocity and pricing rarely miss all at once. Small deviations compound before they become obvious in the bank balance.

Runway is an output, not an explanation

Knowing that runway fell is useful. Knowing which assumption caused it — and what happens if you fix it — is more useful.

Data Processing Agreement

Summary of key terms · Version 1.0 · September 2026

1. Roles

Your company remains the controller of the financial data you connect to Bearing. Bearing acts as a processor and handles that data only to deliver the service you have requested, and only on your documented instructions.

2. Scope of processing

Bearing processes the files and fields you choose to import: financial statements, forecasts, operating metrics and the assumptions behind your plan, together with the account details of the users you invite. Every imported field is shown to you for review before any analysis is run.

3. Purpose limitation

  • Your data is used solely to produce your plan-versus-actual analysis, scenarios and reports.
  • It is never sold, and it is never shared with investors or any third party without an explicit action from you.
  • It is not used to train AI models.

4. Confidentiality and security

Access is restricted to authorised personnel bound by confidentiality obligations. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to customer data is logged.

5. Sub-processors

Bearing relies only on sub-processors bound by data protection terms equivalent to this agreement. The current list is available on request, and you are notified in advance of any change.

6. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect a source or delete an import at any time. On disconnection or at the end of the agreement, the related data is deleted or returned to you, at your choice.

7. Your rights and our assistance

You can access and export your data at any time. Bearing assists you in responding to data subject requests and notifies you without undue delay of any personal data breach affecting your data.

This agreement is structured around Article 28 GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection. The full agreement is countersigned with your company before your first data connection.
