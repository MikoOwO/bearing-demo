The model mattered during the raise. It should still matter after it.

Your bank balance tells you what happened. Your plan tells you what was supposed to happen. Bearing keeps the two connected, month after month.

Plan drift is hard to notice early

Hiring, churn, sales velocity and pricing rarely miss all at once. Small deviations compound before they become obvious in the bank balance.

Runway is an output, not an explanation

Knowing that runway fell is useful. Knowing which assumption caused it — and what happens if you fix it — is more useful.

Investor updates become a separate job

The same monthly analysis can become the basis for an investor update, but the founder decides what is actually shared.

One model. One monthly reality check.
